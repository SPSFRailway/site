Southern Pacific Santa Fe Railway

On July 24th, 1986 the Interstate Commerce Commission made the landmark decision to deny the proposed Southern Pacific Santa Fe Railway merger.

This page is dedicated to one of the greatest "what ifs" in railroad history. A reality where "BNSF" never happens. A world where Union Pacific isn't able to devour another road.
